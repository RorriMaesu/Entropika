Welcome to Entropika!

This tutorial will teach you the basics of survival.

Movement

Use WASD or arrow keys to navigate through the particle field.

Tap or drag on the screen to move toward that location.

Movement costs energy, so be strategic about where you move!

Energy Collection

Collect green energy particles to power your systems (+10 energy each).

Tiny dust particles also provide a small amount of energy.

Collect green energy particles to power your systems (+5 energy each on mobile).

Tiny dust particles provide very little energy (+0.05 each).

Energy is harder to collect on mobile, so be efficient with your movement!

Enemy Avoidance

Red enemy particles will drain your energy and shields!

Special Abilities

Use the Repulsor button (or SPACE key) to push particles away. Each blue energy particle fills the meter a little.

Use the Tractor button (or C key) to suck ALL nearby particles toward you like a magnet (including enemies!). Each purple energy particle fills the meter a little.

You can use abilities with partial energy, but they'll be stronger when the meter is full!

Use the Warp Speed Hail Mary button to launch in a random direction as a last resort escape. Each blue boost particle adds one charge.

Press E to fire weapon (when activated).

Power-ups

Light blue particles add one Warp Speed Hail Mary charge (max 3).

Yellow particles restore shields.

Purple particles give temporary weapon power.

Blue particles add to your repulsor (shield pulse) energy meter.

Dark purple particles add to your tractor beam energy meter.

Collect multiple energy particles to fill your ability meters completely!
