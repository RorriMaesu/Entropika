# Entropika - Complete Tutorial Guide

## Introduction
Welcome to Entropika, an exciting physics-based game where you must navigate through chaotic particle fields, collect energy, and survive increasingly difficult challenges. This tutorial will guide you through all aspects of gameplay.

## Game Objective
Your mission is to collect energy particles to power your ship while avoiding enemy particles that drain your energy. Reach 100 energy points to advance to the next sector, where challenges become progressively more difficult.

## Basic Controls

### Movement
- Use **WASD** or **Arrow Keys** to navigate through the particle field
- Movement costs energy, so be strategic about when and where you move
- You start with very little energy, so collect green particles quickly to survive

### Energy Management
- **Green particles** (+10 energy): Your primary resource - collect these to increase your energy
- **Red enemy particles** (-20 energy, -1 shield): Avoid these as they drain your energy and damage shields
- The energy bar at the top of the screen shows your current energy level
- You need 100 energy to complete a level

## Special Abilities

### Repulsor (Shield Pulse)
- **Activation**: Press **SPACE** key or click the **Repulsor** button
- **Function**: Pushes all nearby particles away from you
- **Energy**: Requires repulsor energy (blue meter)
- **Collection**: Blue particles add to your repulsor energy meter
- The ability is stronger when the meter is full, but can be used with partial energy

### Tractor Beam
- **Activation**: Press **C** key or click the **Tractor** button
- **Function**: Pulls all nearby particles toward you (including enemies!)
- **Energy**: Requires tractor beam energy (purple meter)
- **Collection**: Dark purple particles add to your tractor beam energy meter
- Use carefully as it will attract both helpful and harmful particles

### Warp Speed Hail Mary
- **Activation**: Click the **Warp Speed Hail Mary** button
- **Function**: Launches you in a random direction as an emergency escape
- **Charges**: Each light blue boost particle adds one charge (maximum 3)
- **Invulnerability**: You're invulnerable while the boost is active
- Use this as a last resort when surrounded by enemies

## Power-ups

### Shield Particles
- **Appearance**: Yellow particles
- **Effect**: Adds +1 shield
- Shields protect you from enemy collisions
- Your current shield count is displayed in the HUD

### Boost Particles
- **Appearance**: Light blue particles
- **Effect**: Adds one Warp Speed Hail Mary charge
- Maximum of 3 charges can be stored

### Weapon Power-up
- **Appearance**: Purple particles
- **Effect**: Gives temporary weapon power
- **Activation**: Press **E** key when weapon is active
- Allows you to destroy enemy particles

## Enemy Mechanics
- Enemies actively chase you when you get close
- They have shields that pop on first collision
- After losing their shield, enemies explode on second collision
- Explosions cause double damage if you're within the blast radius

## Environmental Hazards

### Black Holes
- Appear randomly once every minute and last for 11 seconds
- Have stronger gravity affecting everything across the entire map
- Gravity is strongest near the event horizon, making escape difficult
- Abilities cost more energy when used inside a black hole's influence
- Use your Warp Speed Hail Mary to escape if necessary

## Level Progression
- Complete a level by reaching 100 energy
- Each new level introduces:
  - More intelligent enemies
  - Faster-moving particles
  - More challenging environmental hazards
  - Higher energy costs for abilities

## Tips for Success
1. Prioritize collecting green energy particles, especially at the start
2. Use the repulsor ability to push away approaching enemies
3. Save your Warp Speed Hail Mary charges for emergency situations
4. Be strategic with the tractor beam - it can pull in enemies along with helpful particles
5. Watch for the black hole warning and prepare to escape its pull
6. Collect shield particles whenever possible to increase your survivability
7. Movement costs energy, so sometimes it's better to stay still and let particles come to you

Good luck, and master the physics to survive the chaos!
