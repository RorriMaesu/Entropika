# Entropika

Entropika is an interactive particle physics game where players navigate through a mesmerizing environment of particles with unique behaviors and physics properties. Collect energy particles, avoid dangerous entities, and use special abilities to manipulate the physics of the world around you.

## Play Online

You can play Entropika online at: [https://rorrimaesu.github.io/Entropika](https://rorrimaesu.github.io/Entropika)

## Game Features

- **Dynamic Particle System**: Experience a vibrant universe of colorful particles with realistic physics and emergent behaviors
- **Intuitive Controls**: Easy to learn controls that work across desktop and mobile devices
- **Special Abilities**: Use repulsor fields, tractor beams, and warp boosts to navigate challenging environments
- **Responsive Design**: Seamless gameplay experience across desktop and mobile devices in both portrait and landscape orientations
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Challenges increase as you collect more energy and progress through levels

## Gameplay

- **Green particles**: Collect these energy particles to increase your score and energy
- **Red particles**: Avoid these dangerous particles as they will damage your ship
- **Blue particles**: Special particles with unique behaviors
- **Yellow particles**: Power-ups that grant bonuses

## Controls

### Desktop
- **Mouse movement**: Move your ship
- **Key Q/Space**: Activate repulsor field
- **Key E**: Activate tractor beam
- **Key W/Shift**: Activate warp boost

### Mobile
- **Touch and drag**: Move your ship
- **Ability buttons**: Tap the on-screen buttons to activate your special abilities

## Technical Details

Entropika is built using:
- HTML5 Canvas
- Vanilla JavaScript
- CSS3 for responsive design
- Device orientation detection for mobile play

No external libraries or frameworks are used, making it a lightweight and performant web game that works across devices.

## Development

To run the game locally:

2. Open `index.html` in a web browser
